Adj. off and Adj. def are points scored and allowed per 100 possessions, adjusted for the opponents each team has faced and for home court (a ridge regression over every game, the same method as the football EPA ratings). Adj. net is the difference. Net is the raw version. SRS is the same idea in points per game: how many points better than an average team on a neutral floor. SoS is the strength of schedule in points. The last eight columns are the Four Factors on offense and defense.
