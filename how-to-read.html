How to Read Matchups

Basketball is scored by possessions, so the pages measure teams per 100 possessions as well as per game. A fast team scores more per game without being better; per 100 possessions takes pace out.

The board

  • Spread and total are ESPN's current line, loaded in your browser. "Opened" shows the first line the site saw that day when it has moved half a point or more.
  • Implied score splits the total by the spread: the favorite gets half the total plus half the spread.
  • B2B marks a team on the second night of a back-to-back. 3d rest marks a team with three or more days off.
  • Net under each team is its adjusted net rating: points per 100 possessions better than its opponents, adjusted for schedule and home court.

The game header

The line, total and implied score, then the rest spot (days off for each team and games in the last seven days) and altitude for games in Denver and Utah. Tired legs and thin air both show up late in games.

Head to Head

Each offense against the other defense, stat by stat, with league ranks. The longer bar has the better rank. A bar turns team color only when that side is at least one league standard deviation better, so a colored bar is a real gap, not a few rank spots.

  • Adj. rating / 100: points scored (offense) or allowed (defense) per 100 possessions, adjusted for opponents and home court. The best single number for team strength.
  • The Four Factors explain why a team scores. eFG% is shooting, counting a three as 1.5 makes. Turnover % is turnovers per 100 possessions. Offensive rebound % is the share of its own misses a team gets back, faced with the defense's defensive rebound %. FT rate is free throw attempts per 100 shots.
  • Shooting: how many shots are threes (3PA rate), how many go in, and paint points. A defense that gives up threes against a team that lives on them is a matchup to watch.

Team cards

  • SRS (Simple Rating System) is points better than an average team on a neutral floor. OSRS and DSRS split it into offense and defense. SoS is how strong the schedule has been, in points.
  • Players are the top ten by minutes, per game. PRA is points plus rebounds plus assists. Last 5 pts shows recent form. "Out last game" marks a player listed out (not a coach's decision) in the team's last game. Click a name for the last 15 games.
  • Defense vs guards, forwards and centers is what opponents at each position have scored per game against this team (ESPN's position labels), ranked 1 for the fewest points allowed.

Limits

Early in the season every number moves a lot; the adjusted ratings lean toward average until there are enough games. Injury reports are not on the site yet, so check who is out before tip-off.
